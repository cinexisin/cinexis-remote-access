# Cinexis Remote Access — Home Assistant Add-on

Secure remote access to your Home Assistant — plus **Alexa, Google Home & Siri voice control** and **WhatsApp control** — via **Cinexis Cloud**.

- ✅ No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, works behind CGNAT
- ✅ Encrypted TLS tunnel — your HA at `https://{your-id}.ha1.cinexis.cloud`
- ✅ Voice control: Amazon Alexa, Google Home, Siri Shortcuts
- ✅ Control your home from a WhatsApp chat
- ✅ **3-day free trial — no credit card**

## Quick start

1. **Add the repository.** In Home Assistant go to
   **Settings → Add-ons → Add-on Store → ⋮ → Repositories**, paste the URL below and click **Add**:
   ```
   https://github.com/cinexisin/cinexis-remote-access
   ```
   [![Open your Home Assistant instance and add this add-on repository.](https://my.home-assistant.io/badges/supervisor_add_addon_repository.svg)](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/supervisor_add_addon_repository/?repository_url=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2Fcinexisin%2Fcinexis-remote-access)

2. **Install** *Cinexis Remote Access*, then **Start** the add-on.

3. **Open the Cinexis panel** from the Home Assistant sidebar and enter your name + email.
   Your **3-day Pro trial starts instantly** — every feature unlocked, no credit card. Your secure
   remote URL appears right away.

4. **Add voice control (optional).** In the Alexa app: **More → Skills & Games → search “Cinexis” →
   Enable → Link Account** (use the same email, confirm with the one-time code), then say
   *“Alexa, discover devices.”* Google Home and Siri link the same way.

5. **Keep it after the trial.** Open the panel, pick a plan, and pay by UPI or card — a GST invoice
   is issued automatically. No auto-debit; you renew with a tap.

> **Full step-by-step guide with screenshots:** https://cinexis.cloud/setup

## Requirements

Home Assistant **OS** or **Supervised** (so the Add-on Store is available).
